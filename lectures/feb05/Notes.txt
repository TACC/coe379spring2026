================ Random comments

Please do not quit your editor every time you compile:
- use Control-z to suspend the editor process
- do `fg' on the commandline to foreground the editor again

MPI is machine independent:
- your favorite package manager probably has a `mpich' port
- you can download the "reference implementation" from mpich.org and compile it yourself.

The makefile in exercises/exercises-mpi-whatever has an option:

make whatever ECHO=1

shows you precisely what it does.
The `tools.c' file is used in some exercises for the final check.
